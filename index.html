Welcome to Willow & Water

Southern Louisiana beauty without the ballroom feel.

Willow & Water is imagined as the kind of place that feels familiar the moment you arrive — water nearby, shade from the trees, warm evening light, and room for the people who matter most. It is a relaxed Mandeville-area setting for couples who want their wedding to feel personal, graceful, and unmistakably Southern.
